## 1. Summary of What We Collect and Why

TimeSlice is a personal productivity, time-blocking, and focus timer application. We collect only the minimum personal information required to authenticate your identity, save your schedule, track your focus sessions, and deliver requested notifications.

* We **do not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.  
* We **do not** display third-party advertisements or integrate advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob).  
* We **do not** collect location data, address book contacts, financial information, microphone audio, or biometric/health data.  
* We **do not** integrate third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

### A. Account & Authentication Information
* **Email Address:** Collected when you create an account or sign in. Used as your account identifier, for secure login authentication, and to send password reset emails.
* **Firebase Authentication UID:** A unique user identifier generated by Google Firebase Authentication upon account creation. Used to securely associate your productivity records with your authenticated account.
* **Password:** Managed securely and exclusively by Google Firebase Authentication using industry-standard password hashing. TimeSlice never sees, transmits, or stores plain-text passwords on any server.

### B. User Profile Information
* **Display Name:** Optional name or alias you choose to provide in your profile settings.
* **Profile Picture (Avatar):** If you choose to upload a profile photo, the image is compressed and stored directly as a Base64-encoded string within your private user document in Google Cloud Firestore (`users/{userId}.avatarBase64`). **Firebase Cloud Storage is not used or provisioned by TimeSlice.**

### C. Productivity, Activity & Focus Data
* **Scheduled Activities:** Activity title, category (e.g., Work, Study, Health), scheduled date, start time, end time, duration, color tag, icon name, and completion status. Stored in your private Firestore subcollection (`users/{userId}/activities`).
* **Focus Sessions:** Timer session duration, completion timestamp, focus mode/technique, and associated activity references. Stored in your private Firestore subcollection (`users/{userId}/focusSessions`).
* **User Preferences & Goals:** Daily focus target hours (default 4 hours), notification settings, activity reminder toggles, reminder lead minutes (e.g., 5, 10, 15, 30 minutes), sound alert toggles, and vibration alert toggles. Stored in your user document (`users/{userId}`).

### D. Device Tokens & Local Notification Data
* **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token:** An installation-specific device registration token provided by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to route push notifications. Stored in your user document (`users/{userId}.fcmToken`).
* **Local Scheduled Reminders:** Managed directly on your device via the local notification service (`@notifee/react-native`) to trigger timely reminders before scheduled activities begin.

### E. Local Device Storage
* **Active Focus Session State:** Stored locally on your device via React Native AsyncStorage (`focus_session_state`) so that your active focus timer persists if the app is backgrounded, restarted, or interrupted.

## 3. Device Permissions Requested

TimeSlice requests only permissions necessary for core application features:

* **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Requested only when you explicitly tap the camera option to take a profile picture. Never accessed in the background or for any other purpose.
* **Photo Library / Storage / Read Media Images:** Requested only when you explicitly tap the photo gallery option to choose an existing profile picture.
* **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Requested on Android 13+ (API 33+) to deliver scheduled activity reminders and timer completion alerts.
* **Vibrate (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used to produce haptic vibration feedback when a focus timer finishes or when an activity reminder triggers (if enabled in your preferences).

## 4. How Your Data Is Stored and Protected

### Security & Data Transmission
* **Data Transmission Security:** Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections between the TimeSlice mobile application and Google Firebase backend services.
* **Owner-Only Firestore Security Rules:** Your data in Google Cloud Firestore is protected by strict, server-enforced security rules. Database access rules ensure that a user can only read, write, update, or delete records where `request.auth.uid == userId`. No other user can access your activities, focus history, profile, or settings.
* **Infrastructure Security:** Backend data is hosted in Google Cloud data centers maintained under industry-standard compliance and security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3).

## 6. Data Retention Policy

* **Active Accounts:** We retain your account credentials, activities, focus sessions, avatar data, and preferences for as long as your account remains active.
* **Application-Level Deletion:** When your account is deleted (either via the in-app deletion button or upon verification of a manual deletion request), TimeSlice immediately and permanently deletes your user record, authentication credentials, scheduled activities, focus sessions, avatar data, and preferences from Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore. TimeSlice does not retain application-level copies or shadow profiles of deleted accounts.
* **Third-Party Infrastructure Retention & Backups:** Routine system backups, disaster-recovery snapshots, or server logs maintained by Google Cloud / Firebase may retain residual copies of deleted data for a temporary period in accordance with Google's standard cloud data retention schedules (typically up to 30 to 60 days before complete overwriting), over which TimeSlice has no direct operational control. For more details, refer to [Google Cloud's Data Deletion Guidelines](https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms).

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to request or perform complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

### Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Self-Service)
You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app at any time without contacting support:
1. Open TimeSlice and navigate to the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll down to the **Account & Privacy** section.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your choice. If prompted, re-enter your current password to verify ownership.
5. All your data (Firestore user record, activities, focus sessions, avatar Base64, and Firebase Auth account) will be immediately and irreversibly deleted. Your local device cache is purged and your FCM registration token is invalidated.

### Method 2: External Deletion Request (Without Needing the App)
If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request manual deletion by emailing the developer:
* **Email:** [deshrajstack@gmail.com](mailto:deshrajstack@gmail.com)
* **Subject:** `TimeSlice Account Deletion Request`
* **Verification:** Please send the request from the email address registered with your TimeSlice account so account ownership can be verified.
* **Process & Timeline:** Email deletion requests are reviewed and processed manually by the developer (they are not processed automatically by the mobile app). Upon verification, the developer manually deletes your Firebase Authentication account and all associated Cloud Firestore documents and subcollections within **30 days** (and typically within 48 to 72 hours). You will receive a confirmation email once your account and all associated records have been permanently erased.

For complete details, please refer to our [Account Deletion Instructions](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md).
