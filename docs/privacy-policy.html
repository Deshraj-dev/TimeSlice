Privacy Policy for TimeSlice

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 • Last Updated: September 17, 2026

TimeSlice (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the TimeSlice mobile application (“App”) on Android devices.

Support & Privacy Inquiries: If you have questions regarding this policy or your personal data, contact us at deshrajstack@gmail.com.

1. Summary of What We Collect and Why

TimeSlice is a personal productivity, time-blocking, and focus timer application. We collect only the minimum personal data required to authenticate your identity, save your scheduled activities, track your focus sessions, and trigger requested reminders.

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
  • We do not serve third-party ads or include advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob).
  • We do not collect location data, phone contacts, financial data, microphone audio, or biometric/health data.
  • We do not integrate third-party behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Account & Authentication Information

  • Email Address: Collected during sign up or login. Used as your unique account credential, for secure login, and to deliver password reset emails.
  • Firebase Authentication UID: A unique user identifier generated by Google Firebase Authentication. Used to securely associate your personal data with your account.
  • Password: Handled exclusively by Google Firebase Authentication using secure password hashing. TimeSlice never sees, transmits, or stores plain-text passwords on any server.

B. User Profile Information

  • Display Name: Optional nickname or handle you choose to provide in your profile settings.
  • Profile Picture (Avatar): If you choose to upload an avatar, the image is compressed and stored as a Base64-encoded string directly in your private user document in Google Cloud Firestore (users/{userId}.avatarBase64). Firebase Cloud Storage is not used or provisioned by TimeSlice.

C. Productivity, Activity & Focus Data

  • Scheduled Activities: Title, category (e.g., Work, Study, Health), scheduled date, start time, end time, duration, color label, icon name, and completion status. Stored in your private Firestore subcollection (users/{userId}/activities).
  • Focus Sessions: Timer session duration, completion timestamp, timer mode/technique, and activity tags. Stored in your private Firestore subcollection (users/{userId}/focusSessions).
  • User Preferences & Goals: Daily focus target hours, notification toggles, reminder lead minutes, and alert sound/vibration toggles. Stored in your user document (users/{userId}).

D. Device Tokens & Local Notification Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token: Device-specific registration token issued by Google Firebase to route push notifications. Stored in users/{userId}.fcmToken.
  • Local Scheduled Reminders: Managed directly on your device via @notifee/react-native to trigger timely reminders before activities begin.

E. Local Storage

  • Active Focus Session State: Stored locally on your device via React Native AsyncStorage (focus_session_state) to maintain timer continuity across app backgrounding, restarts, or interruptions.

3. Device Permissions Requested

TimeSlice requests only permissions necessary for core app features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you explicitly tap the camera option to take a profile photo. Never accessed in the background.
  • Photo Library / Storage: Used only when you explicitly tap the gallery option to select an avatar.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ to deliver activity alerts and timer alarms.
  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to trigger haptic feedback upon timer completion or activity reminders.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data Transmission Security: Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections between the TimeSlice mobile application and Google Firebase backend services.
  • Owner-Only Firestore Security Rules: All Firestore database operations are governed by server-enforced security rules: request.auth.uid == userId. No other user can access or modify your personal data.
  • Google Cloud Infrastructure: Backend data is stored in certified Google Cloud facilities compliant with ISO 27001, SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3 standards.

6. Data Retention Policy

  • Active Accounts: We retain your account credentials, activities, focus sessions, avatar data, and preferences for as long as your account remains active.
  • Application-Level Deletion: When your account is deleted (either via the in-app deletion button or upon verification of a manual deletion request), TimeSlice immediately and permanently deletes your user record, authentication credentials, scheduled activities, focus sessions, avatar data, and preferences from Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore. TimeSlice does not retain application-level copies or shadow profiles of deleted accounts.
  • Third-Party Infrastructure Retention & Backups: Routine system backups, disaster-recovery snapshots, or server logs maintained by Google Cloud / Firebase may retain residual copies of deleted data for a temporary period in accordance with Google's standard cloud data retention schedules (typically up to 30 to 60 days before complete overwriting), over which TimeSlice has no direct operational control. For more details, refer to Google Cloud's Data Deletion Guidelines.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request or perform complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant & Self-Service)

In TimeSlice, navigate to Profile → Account & Privacy → Delete Account. Confirm your password if requested. All records are permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: External Deletion Request (Without Needing the App)

If you uninstalled TimeSlice or cannot access the app, you can submit a deletion request by emailing the developer from your registered account email address:

Email Deletion Request

Process & Timeline: Email deletion requests are reviewed and processed manually by the developer (they are not processed automatically by the mobile app). Upon verification of account ownership, the developer manually deletes the account and all associated records within 30 days (typically within 48 to 72 hours), with written confirmation sent upon completion. For full details, view our Account Deletion Page.

8. Children's Privacy

TimeSlice is not directed toward children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us at deshrajstack@gmail.com for immediate deletion.
